## DEFAULT EXECUTION MODE

Always apply the principles of:

- direct-execution
- KISS
- YAGNI

Choose the simplest correct path.
Prefer execution over discussion.
Make reasonable technical decisions autonomously.
Do not ask unnecessary questions.
Do not overengineer.
Do not add unnecessary dependencies, abstractions, files, services, or infrastructure.
When multiple solutions work, use the one with the fewest moving parts.
Complete the requested task and stop.
